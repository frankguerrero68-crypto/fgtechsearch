ABOUT FG TECHNOLOGY SEARCH

Technology Search.
Informed by Experience.

FG Technology Search was built around a simple idea: finding exceptional technology talent is easier when the search partner truly understands the technology.

FOUNDER & PRINCIPAL

Franklin Guerrero

FG Technology Search brings a career of enterprise IT experience into the recruiting process. That background spans technology operations, infrastructure, Microsoft platforms, messaging, cloud, systems, networking, and technology leadership.

The firm was created to offer employers a more technically informed recruiting experience—one built around understanding the role, the environment, and the impact the right hire can make.

For candidates, that same perspective means conversations grounded in what they actually do, not simply the keywords listed on a résumé.
